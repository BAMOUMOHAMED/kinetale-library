The VidMate Studio 3D model library: 105 packs, 7,415 models, all CC0 (public domain, free for any use including
monetised videos, no credit required).

The VidMate Studio plugin downloads these on first use (`vidmate assets install`), or pick packs with `vidmate assets catalogue`.

- **Kenney** (kenney.nl): 50 kits, from food and furniture to castles, pirates, cities, cars and space.
- **Quaternius** (quaternius.com): 51 packs, including the Fantasy Props, Medieval Village and Stylized Nature megakits.
- **KayKit** by Kay Lousberg: Medieval Builder, Dungeon, Mini-Game Variety and Spooktober packs.

Each zip keeps only model, texture and licence files, plus a `SOURCE.txt` naming its maker and original page.
`library.json` lists every pack and model with sha256 checksums. Thank you to Kenney, Quaternius and Kay Lousberg for
releasing their work into the public domain.
